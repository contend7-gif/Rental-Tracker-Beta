# Year-end tax checklist QA

- Open Tax Center for the current year. Resolve now separates payment verification, receipt support, tax sign-off and current loan/escrow cleanup from Awaiting annual documents.
- A receipt-backed expense without a bank match still appears under Payment verification. A bank-matched expense without receipt support still appears under Receipt support. Mileage does not require a cash-payment match.
- Before year-end, an unreceived annual loan review can wait, while missing payment months and escrow issues remain actionable. A received annual document or a completed tax year moves outstanding annual reviews to Resolve now.
- Search Transactions for a single already-reviewed purchase, then open Tax Center. Other scoped transaction warnings must remain visible. Clear the search and confirm readiness counts do not change.
- Check year/property/unit scope, checklist navigation, empty states, desktop layout and narrow-window wrapping. Waiting and payment checks must not automatically change tax-review flags or amounts.
